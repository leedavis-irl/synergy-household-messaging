Privacy Policy - Synergy Household Messaging

Synergy is the personal household assistant of Lee Davis of Berkeley, California. This page is the privacy policy for the Synergy Household Messaging program, a private text messaging program that sends conversational messages to household members and household service providers for scheduling and household coordination.

Information we collect

We collect the mobile phone number you use to message with the program and the content of the messages you exchange with the program.

How we use it

We use this information only to operate the household messaging program: to send you household coordination messages you have agreed to receive and to respond to your replies.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Mobile information and messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent, or otherwise share mobile phone numbers, text messaging originator opt-in data, or messaging consent with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.
